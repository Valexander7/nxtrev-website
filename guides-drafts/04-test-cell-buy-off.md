- One vendor owns tester, handler, docking, tooling and on-site qualification to a verified acceptance.
- Before delivery, NXTREV pre-verifies prober or handler docking, load board mechanical tolerance and socket contact pressure.
- Buy-off means the cell passes correlation, Gage R&R and yield buy-off on the customer's actual production silicon.
- NXTREV's field service engineers own line calibration, Gage R&R and sustaining preventive maintenance.
- The site claims qualification cycles drop "from months to weeks" with pre-verification. **This needs a real example before it is repeated in a guide.**
