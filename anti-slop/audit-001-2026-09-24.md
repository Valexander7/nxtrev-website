# antislop audit 001 (2026-09-24)

Scope: the Apple-craft rebuild on `claude/pensive-turing-chnrjj` (home, products index, 8 family pages, shared header and footer, About, Contact, Line Card, 404).
Mode: after the work. Nothing below has been changed yet. Pick the numbers to fix.

Design Read: B2B equipment distributor site for Philippine fab and OSAT engineers and buyers, restrained industrial style from DESIGN.md. Proposed dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-33: header and footer are stamped into pages by a script.** `tools/sync-chrome.py` rewrites every page's HTML with regex replacement. It works, but it is the pattern R-33 forbids. Fix: move the partials to Jekyll includes (`_includes/header.html`, `{% include header.html root="../" %}`), which GitHub Pages already runs. Trade-off: local preview then needs Jekyll.
2. **R-02: em dashes in visible text.** 19 in total: home 8, About 8, Contact 1, test cells 1, operations 1. All come from the original copy (for example "technologies—from initial specification", "— no multi-vendor finger-pointing"). Fix: replace with commas, colons or full stops. Punctuation only; no words change.
3. **R-36 / C-5: performance claims need a source.** Carried over from the old products page: "customer qualification cycles drop from months to weeks", "under 2-hour changeover times", "Sub-micron defect detection", "RoHS and REACH certified", "Rated for > 500,000 actuations", "Up to 35,000 UPH". Fix: the owner confirms each one against a principal datasheet. Nothing is deleted without that confirmation.
4. **R-17: tester count.** The site says "9,000+ AccoTEST testers delivered"; `todo.md` records a "~10,000 tester shipment milestone". Fix: confirm the current figure with the principal and use one number everywhere.
5. **R-38: new footer line.** "AccoTEST product images are supplied by the principal" was added in this rebuild. Fix: confirm it is true, or reword to "official AccoTEST product imagery".
6. **R-35: RFQ form not clicked through end to end.** The rebuild did not touch the form logic. Checked: menus, the AccoTEST stage and tabs, old-anchor redirects, the `?product=` preselect, and phone overflow on every page. Not checked: an actual Web3Forms submit and its error state. Fix: send one test inquiry.
7. **R-25: one failing contrast pair.** `text-slate-500` on `bg-slate-100` is 4.34:1 (needs 4.5:1). New pages don't use it; About and Contact may. Fix: audit those two pages and use `text-slate-600`.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

8. **R-16: buzzwords in original sourcing copy.** "seamlessly connects businesses with dynamic cross-industry solutions", "agile partner", "comprehensive range", "Seamless ATE docking", "Ultra-modern liquid-cooled test head". Fix: rewrite in plain terms (what is sourced, from where, how fast). This is prose, not spec text.
9. **R-11: pill shapes everywhere.** Buttons, platform tabs, jump links and tags are all `rounded-full`. Fix: keep pills for buttons only; give tabs and jump links an 8px radius.
10. **R-06: uppercase, wide-tracked eyebrows on every section.** Keep them as the site's one typographic motif, with the reason written in DESIGN.md, or drop them where the heading alone is enough (products index "Browse", test cells "Why one owner").
11. **R-08: arrows on text links.** `→` appears after most "more" links. Written reason: an arrow marks a link that leaves the page. Fix: remove it from links that stay on the page.
12. **Consistency: About and Contact bodies still use the old styling.** The shared header and footer and the emoji fixes are in, but their sections still use the old cards, spacing, `scrub-rise` leftovers and inline style blocks. Fix: rebuild both on the Craft Layer (`.section`, the heading scale, tables).
13. **Liveliness: dials not recorded.** DESIGN.md has no dial line. Fix: add `Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

14. **R-05: How we work has three numbered steps.** This is the real three-part method, not template filler, and uses no round icons. Reason to record: the content really has three stages.
15. **R-14: eight identical product tiles on home.** The families are parallel choices, so identical tiles are intended. Reason to record, or give the flagship (test cells) a double-width tile.
16. **R-15: "Explore Equipment Catalog" hero button.** Specific enough, but "See all equipment" avoids the stock verb.
17. **R-32: mobile menu doesn't close on Escape.** The desktop Products menu does. Add the same handler.
18. **R-03: platform tabs clip on narrow phones.** "STS8200 CROSS" scrolls horizontally at 390px. Shorten the labels to model numbers, or stack the tabs.
19. **Line Card and 404** keep their own headers. The line card is a print sheet, so that is deliberate. The 404 page draws the logo as styled text; switch it to the SVG logo.
20. **R-21: no dark mode toggle.** The light theme follows DESIGN.md, which is a brand reason. Record it in DESIGN.md.
