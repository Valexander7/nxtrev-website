Operating Standards

Our 5 Core Values

Anchored in observable behaviors, not slogans.

01 • Recommendation

Fit Before Sale

Recommend what suits the application. Clarify acceptance criteria and reject poor fit early.

02 • Accountability

Commit with Clarity

Specific commitments only. Confirm scope, exclusions, dates, and support boundaries in writing.

03 • Execution

Own Follow-Through

Single owner per order. Progress work through verified completion, not just forwarded emails.

04 • Integrity

Tell Truth Early

Surface facts, limits, and uncertainties while action is still possible. Separate facts from forecasts.

05 • Relationship

Build Beyond Sale

Strengthen continuing support depth, document reusable learning, and protect the installed base.
