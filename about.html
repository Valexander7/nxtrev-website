How we work

Fit, then integrate, then stay

The three disciplines behind every engagement below.

  1. 01 · FIT

    Qualify before you quote

    We check the requirement against the application and reject poor fit early, so you buy what actually works on your line.

  2. 02 · INTEGRATE

    Dock, commission, buy off

    One vendor owns tester, handler, docking, tooling, and on-site qualification to a verified acceptance. No multi-vendor finger-pointing.
